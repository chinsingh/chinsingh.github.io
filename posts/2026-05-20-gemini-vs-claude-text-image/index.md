## Reading Images

Asked Claude for font recommendations evoking a certain feel. It did give me some recs, but its widgets got rendered with some fallback fonts. But I actually ended up liking those fallback fonts better. So I asked it, what are those. Not surprisingly, it couldn't figure that out correctly. Although, it pretended. 

So I tried taking a screenshot of the font, and asking. It again confidently pointed me to an incorrect font - which was unexpected. Claude is usually better at self-audit. 

Anyway, I have a good impression of Google at image recognition so I moved to Gemini for this, and here's its response:

> The font used in the image is **Apple Chancery**.
> 
> It is a script font designed by Kris Holmes and is bundled natively with macOS and iOS.
> 
> ### Key Identifying Features:
> - **The Capital 'S' and 'H':** The distinct fluid, calligraphic loops on the uppercase letters are a signature of this typeface. 
>   - **The Lowercase 'g' and 'y':** They feature long, elegant, sweeping descenders that curve sharply to the left.
> - **The Capital 'D' and 'L':** In the second line ("**D**etective, **L**ondon"), you can see the distinct script flourish on the entry strokes.
>
> If you are looking for a highly accessible or cross-platform alternative that looks very similar, **Zapf Chancery** (or its open-source equivalent, **URW Chancery**) is the closest match.


It recognized the defining features and strokes, and suggested alternatives as well.
